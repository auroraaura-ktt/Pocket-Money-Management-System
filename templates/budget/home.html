How It Works

  1. Add Total Money — Enter the total amount of money available for the month.
  2. Set Category Budgets — Enter estimated amounts for each category (e.g. Days 1-10, Days 11-20, Days 21-end, or custom categories like Shopping).
  3. Track Daily Expenses — Record spending each day; it is grouped into the matching category automatically.
  4. Review Reports — Compare each category's budget estimate against actual spending (deficit/surplus).
  5. Monthly Summary — Compare total actual spending against your total money and receive an email report.
